# Screenshots to take

Take these from the **demo** app (atelier-beirut.vercel.app), never the real one.

Save each as a **PNG** in `guide/screenshots/` with **exactly** the filename in
the left column — the document looks them up by name.

Taking a shot:
- **Laptop:** `Windows key + Shift + S`, drag around the browser window, then
  paste into Paint and save as PNG.
- **Phone:** normal screenshot, then send it to yourself on WhatsApp and save
  it on the laptop.

## Two tips

**Sign out of anything personal first.** Browser tabs, bookmarks bar,
notifications — crop them out or hide them. A screenshot with your WhatsApp
notifications in it looks careless.

**Take the phone ones on a real phone**, not a resized browser window. The
proportions are different and people notice.

---

When the files are in place, open `guide/guide.html` in Chrome →
**Print** → Destination **Save as PDF** → **Save**.

Any screenshot you haven't taken yet shows as a dashed box with its filename,
so you can print a draft at any point and see what's still missing.
